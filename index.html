One line per speech, like CHARACTER: what they say. Stage directions in (parentheses) or [brackets] are shown but not spoken. Laughing cues play canned audience laughter; beep cues play a quick burst of beeps. Gasps play an audience gasp; shuffling, footsteps and chair movement play shuffling sounds. Cues for everyone, such as (Everyone laughs.) or ALL: (laugh), light up the whole cast and stay on screen a little longer. Paste the whole play, then press Load.
